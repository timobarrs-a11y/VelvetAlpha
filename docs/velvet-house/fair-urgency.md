# Fair urgency: how time pressure works across Velvet Sims

*Design rules. Applies to every system that has a deadline, a timer or a thing that can go wrong.*

The aim is urgency the player can see coming and act on, without the feeling of having been robbed. Players
accept pressure when they can read it and choose how to meet it. They quit when something disappears with no
warning or no way back.

## The rules

1. **Warn before anything is lost.** A deadline, a spoiling item or a due bill is shown in advance, in plain words
   ("the seed needs water tomorrow", "offer closes tonight"), before it matters.
2. **Slow down, don't destroy.** A missed step costs time or money, never the whole thing. A dry bed stops growing,
   and a missed side job goes back into the offers list. The one exception is a crop left dry for two game days: it
   dies. It is only lost after a warning at half that time, and the player clears the bed to plant again.
3. **Say what waiting costs.** Every warning says what the next choice will cost: "two days behind", "$40 late fee".
   The player should never discover a loss after the fact.
4. **Replace what expires.** Expired offers are replaced by new ones. The player keeps a choice; the list doesn't empty.
5. **Progress is never lost silently.** If progress is reset or a decision is undone, the log says why and what was kept.
6. **Needs go down; tasks go up.** Needs (food, energy, fun) decay on their own and are maintenance. Tasks (hobbies,
   jobs, planting) only move when the player engages. Don't make a task decay while the player is away.
7. **Absence rewards, it doesn't punish.** Return hooks are things waiting for the player (offers, a finished batch,
   a paycheque), not things they missed. Avoid timers that run only against the player.
8. **Pace in game time.** Progress follows game minutes, so 1x and fast speed both feel right, and the rules don't
   depend on the real clock.

## Where the rules apply now

| System | Pressure | Warning | What a miss costs | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Side-job offers | Offers expire after two days | Shown on the phone with the closing time | The offer is gone | New offers arrive each morning |
| Bills | Due dates, late fees | Due-soon and due-today states | A late fee | Pay it; the fee stops |
| Food | Fridge runs low | Food count on the HUD | Meals run out | Cook, or order takeout |
| Garden (planned) | Plants need water | Warning the day before | Slower growth | Water it; progress is kept |
| Career review (planned) | Weekly review | Shown in advance | Promotion waits a week | Work on the skill it needs |

## What the rules rule out

- A plant, a skill or a job that is lost or reset without the player choosing it.
- A penalty that stacks on top of a missed deadline (a late fee and a lost offer and a mood drop for one slip).
- Hidden timers. If a number decides something, the player can see it.
