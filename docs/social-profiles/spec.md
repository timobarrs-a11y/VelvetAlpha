# Social Profiles, Progress Feed & Coaching Loop — Spec

Status: draft for review · Owner: Timo · Last updated: 2026-10-20

## 1. What we're building

Open VelvetAlpha up so members can see each other's progress. Every member gets a
public profile (avatar only — no face photos) showing their shared goals and
progress. When they do something — finish a commitment, hit a streak, complete a
plan step — the app posts it for them automatically as a reward. Other members
can like, comment, follow, and discover each other by shared goals or by what's
trending. Atlas can introduce members working on the same things.

The loop this closes: the small retention mechanics (streaks, check-ins,
commitments, milestones) stop being private bookkeeping and become something
other people see and respond to.

Underneath it, the coaching loop has to be solid (§14). From the first
conversation, the coach creates the goal, plan and commitments, walks the user
through them, and edits them live. That's what generates the progress that gets
shared.

### Principles

1. **Action is the content.** Most posts are generated by doing, not writing.
2. **Avatars, never faces.** No photo uploads anywhere on the profile.
3. **Visible by default, invisible by choice.** Every member's profile is
   visible from day 1 so the community feels alive, and anyone can switch to
   invisible. Only the fields in §3 can ever be
   public. Nothing from journals, coaching chat, memories, insights or
   location ever leaves the private layer.
4. **Try first, then pay. Adults only.** New members get 48 hours of full
   access with no card, then a 3-day card trial or membership. Everyone has a
   verified email and a server-verified 18+ date of birth. Every social surface
   is gated server-side (§2). No photo ID: we don't add walls to the experience.
5. **Bots are disclosed.** Bots like and comment as part of the system; the site
   says so, and bot accounts are labeled on their profiles and comments.

## 2. Access: welcome window → card trial → membership

Applies to the whole app, not just social. The goal: let people see what the
product does before asking for a card, without opening the door to abuse.

### Flow

```
Sign up (email + password + date of birth, invisible bot check)
  → verification email → user clicks link
  → WELCOME WINDOW: 48 hours, full access, no card
  → lock screen:
       [Keep going free for 3 more days]  card required, $0 today
       [Start my membership]              pay now, any plan
  → 3-DAY CARD TRIAL → billed for essential on day 3 unless they cancel
                       or choose another plan
```

### Step 1: Signup and email verification

- **Turn on "Confirm email"** in Supabase Auth. The 48-hour clock starts when
  the user clicks the link (`auth.users.email_confirmed_at`), not at signup.
  Nobody burns their window waiting for an email.
- **Create profiles on the server.** A trigger on `auth.users` insert creates
  the `user_profiles` row (terms version and consent timestamps passed through
  signup metadata), replacing the browser insert.
- **Date of birth at signup.** Use the selects from `AgeVerificationModal`.
  - Under 18 is rejected on the spot.
  - It's stored server-side by `verify_age` (see "Date of birth" below).
- **"Check your inbox" screen** with resend (rate-limited to once per 60
  seconds, 5 per hour) and "wrong email? change it".
- **Bot check.** Cloudflare Turnstile in invisible mode on the signup form,
  verified server-side through Supabase Auth's built-in CAPTCHA support. Real
  people never see a challenge.

### Step 2: Welcome window (48 hours, no card)

- **Server-set expiry.** On email confirmation, set
  `user_profiles.welcome_expires_at = email_confirmed_at + 48 hours` and
  `subscription_status = 'welcome'`. Only the server writes these, so clock
  changes or clearing storage don't extend anything.
- **Full access.** Every feature, game, coach, Atlas, and social, with two
  limits:
  - **AI messages:** a daily cap of 200 (in `app_settings`, key
    `welcome_daily_message_cap`). A real person exploring won't hit it; a
    script will within minutes. On hitting it: "You've used today's welcome
    messages — start your trial to keep going."
  - **DMs:** same as every member. DMs to people who aren't connected yet go
    out as message requests (§7).
- **One welcome window per email.**
  - Emails are normalized before the check: lowercased, `+tags` stripped, dots
    removed for Gmail.
  - Signups from disposable-email domains (maintained blocklist) skip the
    window and go straight to the lock screen.
  - Stored in `welcome_grants (email_normalized PRIMARY KEY, user_id,
    granted_at)`, which survives account deletion so a new account with the
    same email doesn't get a second window.
- **Countdown.** A small banner shows "31 hours of full access left". A
  reminder notification and email go out at 6 hours left.

### Step 3: Lock screen

When `welcome_expires_at` passes and there's no trial or subscription:

- A full-screen choice with two options: **"Keep going free for 3 more days"**
  or **"Start my membership."**
- Nothing is deleted. Profile, posts, progress, goals and coach history are all
  kept. Social content is hidden from others until they continue.
- Read-only access to their own data (goals, progress) stays available behind
  the lock screen, so it never feels like their stuff was taken.

### Step 4: 3-day card trial

Replace the `start_trial()` RPC with Stripe Checkout:

- **Checkout settings.** `create-checkout-session` gets a trial mode that sets
  `subscription_data.trial_period_days = 3` and
  `payment_method_collection = 'always'`. Nothing is charged until day 3, and
  the member can cancel any time before then.
- **Plan.** The trial subscribes to the cheapest plan (essential). The member
  gets top-tier features during the trial, then is billed for essential on day 3
  unless they pick another plan or cancel.
- **Webhook.** `stripe-webhook` already syncs tiers on
  `customer.subscription.updated` for `trialing` status. Store
  `subscription_status = 'trialing'` and `trial_expires_at` from the
  subscription's `trial_end`.
- **One trial per account.** Keep `trial_used`.
- **Cleanup.**
  - Retire the client call to `start_trial()` in `PricingPageRoute.tsx`.
  - Fix the toast text.
  - Existing no-card trials keep running until they expire.

### Abuse and cost protections

| Protection | Stops |
|---|---|
| Verified email, one welcome window per normalized email | Throwaway accounts farming free access |
| Invisible Turnstile check on signup | Scripted mass signups |
| Daily AI message cap during the welcome window | One account burning AI budget |
| Per-user rate limit on AI endpoints (existing `rate_limits` table): e.g. 20 messages/minute | Scripts hammering the API |
| Spend alert on the AI provider account + daily cost-by-tier report in the admin console | Abuse going unnoticed until the monthly bill |

### Date of birth, verified server-side

No photo ID.

- Revoke client `UPDATE` on `age_verified_at` and `birthday`.
- Add RPC `verify_age(p_birthdate date)` (SECURITY DEFINER):
  - rejects anyone under 18,
  - writes `birthday` and `age_verified_at`,
  - is write-once (only an admin can correct it).
- Existing users without a server-set DOB see `AgeVerificationModal` the next
  time they open the app.

### Possible-minor reports

- Add a report reason "may be under 18".
- These reports go to the top of the admin console queue.
- An admin can pause the account's social access while reviewing.

### Gate functions

Used by RLS and every edge function:

```sql
has_full_access(uid uuid) RETURNS boolean
-- true when:
--   subscription tier is essential | plus | elite
--   OR subscription_status = 'trialing' with a Stripe subscription
--   OR now() < welcome_expires_at

can_use_social(uid uuid) RETURNS boolean
-- true when:
--   has_full_access(uid)
--   AND age_verified_at IS NOT NULL (set only by verify_age)
--   AND NOT is_banned
--   AND social access not paused by an admin
```

### Lapsed access

When a welcome window, trial or subscription ends:

- profile and posts are hidden from others (not deleted);
- everything comes back as it was on renewal.

## 3. Public profile

**Visible by default.** Every member's profile is visible and discoverable from
day 1 so the community is alive for everyone. Any member can switch to
**invisible**, which hides their profile, posts and discovery listing from
everyone else. They can switch back any time.

Extend the existing `im_discovery_profiles` table, which is already the public
projection used by IM discovery. Do **not** expose `user_profiles`: it stays
owner-read-only.

| Field | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `display_name` | user-entered | Defaults to `nickname`. |
| `avatar_config` | snapshot of `user_profiles.avatar_config` | Rendered with the existing avatar system. Drop or lock `avatar_url` so it can only point at our generated avatar renders, never an upload. |
| `headline` | user-entered | Existing column. |
| `gender` | copied on opt-in | New column plus `show_gender boolean default false`. |
| `interests` | user-picked | Existing column. |
| Shared goals | `social_shared_goals` | See below. |
| Posts | `social_posts` | See §4. |

Nothing else. Public fields are copied into the public table only when the user
saves their profile; there are no views or joins from public tables into
`user_profiles`.

### Shared goals

`social_shared_goals (user_id, goal_id → user_goals, auto_post boolean, show_details boolean, created_at)`

- Sharing is per goal. Nothing is shared by default.
- Public goal fields: `title`, `goal_type`, `current_value`, `target_value`,
  `unit`, `target_date`, `status`, and the streak. `notes` is never public.
- `show_details` (default false) controls whether commitment and plan-step
  titles appear in auto-posts (§4).

## 4. Auto-posts (progress as reward)

### Triggers

Database triggers write an event to `social_post_events`. A worker turns events
into posts. A trigger fires only if the related goal is in
`social_shared_goals` with `auto_post = true`.

| Event | Source |
|---|---|
| Commitment completed | `coaching_commitments.status → 'completed'` (must have `goal_id`) |
| Plan step completed | `coach_plan_steps.completed_at` set (plan linked to a shared goal) |
| Goal completed | `user_goals.status → 'completed'` |
| Goal progress milestone | `user_goals.current_value` crosses 25 / 50 / 75 % |
| Streak milestone | Check-in streak hits 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 100, 180, 365 |
| Achievement unlocked | `milestone_achievements` insert (rarity uncommon or higher) |

Commitments with no `goal_id` never post. They come from chat extraction and
may be private.

### Post content

- Generated from **templates**, not from chat text. Never use
  `coaching_commitments.extracted_from_message`, and use `description` only when
  `show_details = true`.
  - Default example: "Completed a step toward **Run a 5K** · 🔥 12-day streak"
  - With details: "Completed **'Run 3km without stopping'** toward **Run a 5K**"
- The user can add an optional one-line note afterwards.
- Several events on the same goal within 6 hours are bundled into one post.
- At most 3 auto-posts per user per day; overflow is bundled into a daily recap.
- The user's first auto-post shows a one-time toast: "We shared this to your
  profile" with **Undo** and **Turn off for this goal**.
- Any post can be deleted. Deletion is a soft delete, and the post is hidden
  immediately.

### Manual posts

Members can also write a free-text post (optionally linked to a shared goal).
Same moderation as comments (§7).

## 5. Likes

### Behavior

- One reaction type: **like**.
- Public display is the **count only**. Everyone who can see a post sees its
  like count. The list of who liked is not shown to anyone, including the
  author.
- Likes come from members and from bots. Both count the same toward the
  displayed total.

### Data

`social_likes (post_id, liker_id, liker_kind 'human'|'bot', created_at, UNIQUE(post_id, liker_id))`

`liker_kind` is internal: it's used for metrics (§11) and for ranking (§8), and
isn't shown in the UI.

### Bot likes

- **Bot accounts.** Bots are labeled accounts using the existing pattern
  (`auth.users.raw_app_meta_data.is_bot = true`, as with Maya and Jordan in
  `community_bot_infrastructure`). Each has an interest profile.
- **Matching.** A bot likes a post when the post's goal type or interests match
  its profile.
- **Momentum.** Each author has a like budget that grows with:
  - account age and number of posts,
  - real engagement (human likes, comments, follows),
  - the size of the milestone (a 100-day streak earns more than a first
    check-in).

  A first post gets a few likes; budgets ramp over weeks. Default curve:

  | Author's post # | Bot likes per post |
  |---|---|
  | 1–3 | 1–4 |
  | 4–10 | 3–10 |
  | 11–30 | 5–20 |
  | 30+ | Grows with human engagement, up to the per-post cap |

  Big milestones (goal completed, a 30+ day streak) get up to 2× the range. The
  per-post cap defaults to 60.

- **Settings.** The curve, milestone multiplier and cap live in `app_settings`
  under the key `social_bot_like_budget`, so they can be tuned from the admin
  console without a deploy.

- **Delivery.** Likes are spread over hours, front-loaded the way organic
  engagement is, not dropped in all at once.
- **Runner.** A new `social-bot` edge function on cron (service role only),
  modeled on `community-bot`, logs every action to `social_bot_runs`.
- **Exclusions.** Bots never like posts flagged by moderation, posts flagged as
  crisis content (§7), or posts from users who turned bot interactions off.

### Disclosure

- A help/FAQ page ("Bots on VelvetAlpha") explains that bots like posts that
  match their interests and comment with questions.
- The Terms and Privacy Policy get the same paragraph.
- Bot profiles carry a **Bot** badge.

## 6. Question bots

Clearly labeled bot accounts that reply to posts with a probing question, to
get people reflecting and talking.

- Examples: "What made today's run easier than last week's?" or "Day 30 — what's
  one thing you'd tell yourself on day 1?"
- **Input:** the post text and the author's public profile only. No private
  data.
- **Labeling:** comments are stored with `is_ai_generated = true` and shown with
  the Bot badge.
- **Frequency:** at most one bot question per post, on roughly 1 in 3 eligible
  posts, at most 2 per user per day.
- **Never:** on crisis-flagged posts, on users who turned it off, or questions
  that are judgmental or medical/clinical.
- **Notifications:** when the author replies, the bot may answer once. The
  thread is then left for humans.
- **Prompt rules:** prompts live in `src/prompts/` with the other bot
  personalities. Questions are short (≤ 25 words), one per comment, and never
  open with praise filler.

## 7. Comments, moderation, safety

### Comments

`social_comments (id, post_id, author_id, body, is_ai_generated, created_at, deleted_at)`

Who can comment is a per-user setting:

| Setting | Who can comment |
|---|---|
| `everyone` (default) | Any member |
| `mutuals` | People who follow the author and whom the author follows back |
| `nobody` | No one |

### Moderation

- **Pre-screen.** Every human post and comment goes through the existing
  `_shared/moderation.ts` before it is visible. Blocked content is rejected
  with a reason. Borderline content is held and logged to `moderation_flags`.
- **Reports.** Extend `community_reports.target_type` with `social_post`,
  `social_comment` and `profile`, so reports reuse the existing admin console
  queue.
- **Blocks.** `im_blocks` is enforced in RLS on every social table. A blocked
  user can't see the blocker's profile, posts or comments, and can't like,
  comment, follow or DM them, in either direction.

### DMs and message requests

Today only friends can message each other (`im_friendships`).

- **Message requests.** A member can send a message request to anyone whose
  profile they can see: a friend request with a first message attached.
  - The recipient sees it under **Requests** with the message, the sender's
    profile, and **Accept**, **Decline** and **Block**.
  - Accepting opens the conversation. Declining is silent; the sender sees no
    rejection.
  - This applies to every member, including those in the welcome window.
- **Limits.**
  - The request message goes through the same moderation pre-screen as
    comments.
  - Pending requests are capped per sender per day: 10 during the welcome
    window, 30 after, configurable in `app_settings`.
  - Members can turn requests off ("only people I follow can send requests").

### Crisis content

Self-harm or acute-distress signals on a post or comment:

- the post is not boosted, gets no bot likes or bot comments, and doesn't
  appear in discovery;
- the author privately sees support resources.

## 8. Follow & discovery

- **Follows:** `social_follows (follower_id, followee_id, created_at)`.
- **Counts are shown.** Follower and following counts appear on profiles and
  discovery cards: seeing who has momentum draws people in to follow too.
  *(Changed from the original "never shown" decision.)*
- **Discovery, mode A — shared goals.** Cards like "12 people training for a 5K
  right now", built from `social_shared_goals` grouped by `goal_type` and
  normalized title. Tapping a card lists those members' profiles.
- **Discovery, mode B — trending.** Posts ranked by effort and recent
  engagement:
  - ranking signals: milestone size, streak length, human likes, comments, and
    follows from the post;
  - bot likes don't feed ranking, so bots can't decide what trends.
- **Search:** by interest and goal type.
- **Eligibility:** only profiles with `discoverable = true` and
  `can_use_social()` appear anywhere in discovery.

## 9. Atlas introductions

Atlas, not the coaches, makes the social introductions. It sees across all of a
user's coaches and goals, and the Atlas interface is the natural place for
"meet people" suggestions.

- **New tool: `find_people`.** Calls a SECURITY DEFINER function that reads
  only the public layer:
  - `im_discovery_profiles` where `discoverable`,
  - `social_shared_goals` and recent `social_posts`,

  filtered by `can_use_social()` and blocks.
- **Hard rule.** `atlas-agent` currently queries with the service-role client
  (`supabaseAdmin`), which bypasses RLS. The social tool must **not** query
  another user's `user_goals`, `coaching_*`, memories or chats through it. Any
  information about another member comes only from the public layer above.
  Enforce this in code review and with a test that seeds private data for user
  B and asserts it never appears in user A's Atlas context.
- **Suggestion style:** "Sam is also working toward a 5K and just hit a 30-day
  streak. Want to see their profile?"
- **Limits:** at most one unprompted introduction per day. Users can turn
  introductions off, and can't be suggested to others while
  `discoverable = false`.

## 10. Notifications

- Extend `community_notifications.type` with `social_like`, `social_comment`,
  `social_follow` and `atlas_intro`.
- Likes are batched: "Your 14-day streak got 23 likes" at most every few hours
  per post, not one notification per like.

## 12. Metrics

- **Primary:** 30-day retention of members with ≥ 1 human connection (a follow,
  or a human comment or like received) vs. members with none.
- **Bot share of likes** per author over time (internal), so we can see whether
  human engagement is growing or the feed is mostly bot-driven.
- **Auto-posting:** opt-in rate, and undo/turn-off rate on first auto-post.
- **Discovery:** follows and comments that come from Atlas introductions and
  from discovery.
- **Safety:** report rate, block rate, and moderation rejections per 1k posts.

## 14. Coaching loop: conversation → goal → plan → commitments

From the **first** coaching conversation, the coach turns what the user says
into a goal, an action plan and commitments. It then opens the Plan and
Commitments tabs and walks the user through them in an instant-messenger-style
popup. Because the popup is the coach itself (same conversation, same memory),
the user can ask questions or ask for changes, and the coach edits the plan and
commitments live.

This is also the foundation for auto-posts (§4): those depend on every
commitment and step being attached to the right goal.

### Current state (gaps found in the code)

| Finding | Where |
|---|---|
| Coach tools exist: `create_plan` (as a proposal), `add_step`, `adjust_step`, `create_commitment`, `create_artifact`. Mentor coaches get them, with at most 6 tool calls per reply. | `supabase/functions/_shared/coachTools.ts`, `chat-turn/index.ts` |
| No tool to create a goal. Goals are only created by the user through forms. `extract-goal` exists, but nothing calls it. A first conversation can produce a plan attached to no goal. | `goalService.ts`, `supabase/functions/extract-goal` |
| `activeGoalId()` attaches every new plan and commitment to the user's **oldest** active goal, not the goal being discussed. This is wrong as soon as someone has two goals. | `coachTools.ts` |
| Commitments are created two ways: the `create_commitment` tool and a separate extraction step (`parsed.commitment`, confidence > 0.7). The extraction path can create duplicates and doesn't link a goal or step. | `chat-turn/index.ts` ~L827 |
| Commitment status updates (`parsed.commitmentUpdate`) find rows with a fuzzy `ilike` on the description. That can update the wrong commitment, or several at once. | `chat-turn/index.ts` ~L846 |
| No tools to edit a commitment's text or due date, cancel a commitment, remove or reorder steps, edit a plan, or activate a plan. | `coachTools.ts` |
| Plans and commitments live on separate pages, not beside the chat. The thread tab bar has no Plan or Commitments tab. | `CoachingCommitmentsPage`, `CoachingProgressPage`, `GoalDetailPage`, `ThreadTabBar.tsx` |
| `chat-turn` only tells the client about artifacts (`coachArtifacts`). Plan and commitment changes aren't sent back, and nothing subscribes to those tables in realtime, so the UI doesn't update live. | `chat-turn/index.ts` ~L2264 |

### First-conversation flow

1. **Intake (first few exchanges).** The coach learns:
   - what the goal is and why it matters,
   - the timeframe,
   - where the user is now,
   - constraints (time, money, injuries).

   It asks naturally, not as a form.
2. **Create as soon as the basics are known.** Once the coach knows the goal
   and the timeframe, it creates a starter set in the same reply. It doesn't
   wait for perfect information and refines later.
   - `create_goal`
   - `create_plan` with 3–6 steps, each with a concrete quantity and minimum
     (existing rules)
   - `create_commitment` for 1–3 commitments due within the next 7 days, each
     tied to a step
3. **Walkthrough.**
   - The client opens the **Plan** tab with the new items highlighted.
   - The coach appears as an IM-style popup docked over the tab and goes
     through each item: what it is, why that number, and what "minimum" means.
   - The popup then switches to the **Commitments** tab and covers what's due
     first.
4. **Questions and changes.** The user types in the popup.
   - "Why 4 days?" gets an answer.
   - "Can we make it mornings only?" makes the coach edit the step or
     commitment, and the tab updates in front of them.
5. **Lock it in.**
   - The walkthrough ends with **"Let's go"**, which sets the plan from
     `proposed` to `active`.
   - If the user closes the popup instead, the plan activates when they complete
     their first commitment.

The popup is the same coaching thread (`chat-turn`, same companion, same
memory). It is a compact view of the conversation, not a separate bot. Messages
sent in the popup also appear in the main coach chat.

### Coach tools: new and changed

| Tool | Change |
|---|---|
| `create_goal` | **New.** Input: `title`, `goal_type`, and optionally `target_value`, `unit`, `target_date`. Skips duplicates by title and returns `goal_id`. The goal is linked to this coach via new column `user_goals.companion_id`. |
| `create_plan` | Takes `goal_id`. If omitted, uses this coach's goal, and only when the coach has exactly one. Otherwise the call fails and the coach has to say which goal. Replaces the "oldest active goal" fallback. |
| `update_plan` | **New.** Input: `title`, `description`, `cadence`, `status` (`proposed` → `active`, `paused`, `completed`). |
| `remove_step` | **New.** Input: `step_id`, `reason`. Soft delete; the reason is shown in the step history. |
| `reorder_steps` | **New.** Input: `plan_id`, ordered `step_ids`. |
| `create_commitment` | Takes `goal_id`, which otherwise comes from the step's plan. |
| `update_commitment` | **New.** Input: `commitment_id`, plus any of `description`, `due_date`, `plan_step_id`. |
| `complete_commitment` / `cancel_commitment` | **New.** Input: `commitment_id`, plus a `reason` when cancelling. These replace the fuzzy `commitmentUpdate` matching. |
| `show_in_tab` | **New, UI only.** Input: `tab` (`plan` \| `commitments`) and `highlight_ids`. Opens the tab beside the chat and highlights the listed items. |

Rules for all of these:

- **Ownership.** Every id is verified against the user and this coach
  (`ownedPlan` / `ownedStep` exist; add `ownedCommitment` and `ownedGoal`).
- **User-created commitments.** The coach can't cancel these unless the user
  asked in this conversation.
- **Tool budget.** Raise the per-reply cap from 6 to 12 for the first coaching
  session only, since goal, plan and commitments all land in one reply. Keep 6
  after that.
- **One creation path.** Once these tools ship, retire the separate extraction
  path (`parsed.commitment`, `parsed.commitmentUpdate`) so commitments are only
  created and changed through tools.

### Live updates and undo

- **Response payload.** `chat-turn` returns `coachingChanges` (entity type, id,
  action) and `uiActions` (from `show_in_tab`) alongside `coachArtifacts`. The
  client reacts immediately.
- **Realtime.** The client subscribes via Supabase Realtime to `user_goals`,
  `coach_plans`, `coach_plan_steps` and `coaching_commitments` for the signed-in
  user. Changes made anywhere (coach, user, another tab, the overdue sweep)
  show up without a refresh.
- **Highlighting.** Changed items flash with **"Coach updated this"** and an
  **Undo** button.
- **Change log.** Every coach change is written to
  `coaching_change_log (user_id, companion_id, entity_type, entity_id, actor,
  action, before jsonb, after jsonb, reason, created_at)`. This generalises the
  per-step `override_log`.
- **Undo.** Undo restores `before` and tells the coach on its next turn through
  `[COACHING STATE]`.
- **User edits.** Edits in the tabs keep the existing flow: the coach responds
  with `adjust_step` (hold, accept or revise) when an edit goes below the
  minimum.

### Keeping it all in sync

- **Step ↔ commitment.** Completing one completes the other, in both
  directions. Move this into a database trigger so it works however the change
  is made (tab tick, coach tool, or sweep).
- **Goal progress.** Completed commitments update goal progress through the same
  trigger. Today that only happens in the `commitmentUpdate` path.
- **Coaching state.** `[COACHING STATE]` in the coach prompt always includes the
  goal, the active plan with step ids, open commitments, and the most recent
  `coaching_change_log` entries. The coach always knows what changed and who
  changed it.
- **Other features.** Auto-posts (§4), proactive check-ins and weekly reviews
  read from the same tables, so they stay consistent automatically.

### Tests

- **Tool tests.** Extend `src/services/__tests__/coachTools.test.ts`:
  - each new tool,
  - the ownership checks,
  - "no goal guessing when the coach has several goals".
- **End-to-end (Playwright).** New user, first coach conversation:
  - a goal, plan and commitments are created,
  - the Plan tab opens and the popup walks through it,
  - asking for a change updates the tab live,
  - Undo restores the original.
